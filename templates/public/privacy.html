Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ policy_updated }}

This policy explains what information the {{ business_name }} business management system (www.hfflex.com, “the system”) handles and how it is protected.

1. What the system is

The system is a private tool used by {{ business_name }} and its authorised employees to run the business. It is not offered to the general public.

2. Information we handle

  • Business records entered by staff: customer and supplier details, quotations, orders, production, quality, purchasing, inventory and similar records, including contact names, phone numbers and email addresses of business contacts.
  • Employee account details: username, name, department and access permissions, and a securely hashed password.
  • Uploaded files such as documents, images and certificates.
  • Technical data needed to run the site, such as server access and error logs.

3. How we use it

Only to operate {{ business_name }}'s business: managing orders and production, keeping records, and securing and backing up the system. We do not sell information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Cookies

The system uses only the cookies required to keep an employee signed in and to protect forms against forgery. It does not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

5. Google Drive backups

To protect against data loss, the server makes a nightly backup of the system's database, files and configuration. The backup is encrypted on our server before it is uploaded to a Google Drive account owned and controlled by {{ business_name }}. The system accesses Google only through that company-owned account, solely to upload and restore these encrypted backups. It does not access, collect, store or share any other person's Google data, and it does not use Google data for advertising or sell it. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

6. Where data is stored and who can see it

Data is stored on servers operated for {{ business_name }} with commercial hosting providers. Access is limited to authorised employees and to administrators who maintain the system. We do not share business records with third parties except where required by law or needed to provide hosting and backup services as described above.

7. Security

We protect the system with password sign-in, HTTPS encryption in transit, restricted server access, firewalls and encrypted backups. No system can be guaranteed completely secure, but we work to keep the information safe.

8. Retention

Business records are kept for as long as the business needs them. Nightly database backups are kept for about 14 days and configuration backups for about 60 days, after which they are deleted.

9. Your choices

If you are an employee or a business contact and want to see, correct or delete information about you, contact us at {{ contact_email }}.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.

11. Contact
